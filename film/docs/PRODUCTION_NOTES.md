# THE HEART IN THE ICE — production notes

**What it is:** a 69.8 s vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) explainer. In 1965 the CIA and India's Intelligence Bureau
carried a plutonium-powered listening post up Nanda Devi to catch radio telemetry from Chinese missile tests. They
lost it. Every frame, sound and voice line is generated by the code in this folder. There is no stock footage, no
sample library and no music.

## 1. Story research and fact ledger

disagree, the film uses the wording common to all of them.

## 2. Reference analysis → decisions

* **Vertical explainer reels (cream/purple):** word-synced captions with a small italic lead-in and a heavy keyword,
  one visual metaphor per beat, cuts every 2–4 s, and a slow camera push on every shot.
  *Adopted* the caption hierarchy and the beat density. *Surpassed* them with real-data visuals (DEM terrain, the
  real elevation profile, real river geometry) instead of clip-art metaphors.
* **Product promo (glossy 3D):** motion blur on fast moves, light leaks, depth-of-field reveals, and transitions
  hidden inside the motion. *Adopted* zoom-blur match cuts, motion blur and bloom/halation. *Rejected* the glossy
  look, which doesn't fit an intelligence story.

## 3. Design system

* **Two worlds.** NIGHT (field reality: ice, terrain, signals) and PAPER (analysis: diagrams, records). The film
  alternates between them for rhythm.
* **Palette.** Ink #15171B · Paper #ECE5D6 · Night #06080B → #18212B · Snow #E8EDF1 · Steel #93A3B3 ·
  Signal ice #8FD3FF (signal ink on paper #296B8F) · China/danger red #E5452F · Plutonium amber #FFB04A, core #FFE9C2.
  Amber is reserved for the device: it is the thread the eye follows through every scene.
* **Type.** Instrument Serif Italic (lead-ins), Inter 800 (keywords), Courier Prime (typewriter records),
  JetBrains Mono (HUD), Old Standard (1978 newsprint), Noto Serif SC (中国 / 罗布泊).
* **Motion.** Long-tail Bezier ease-outs (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for camera moves, expo-out for reveals, damped springs
  for paper settling. Every shot keeps a slow push or drift, so no frame is ever static.
* **Transitions motivated by content.** Ice core → map dot (match cut with zoom blur); map → paper sheet slid over
  it; diagram summit → 3D summit (zoom-through); blueprint → darkness around the glowing capsules → the carried
  amber dot; blizzard whiteout → crevice; avalanche dust → search; Ganges source → back into the ice (bookend).
* **Film finish.** Quarter-res bloom pyramid, red-orange halation, radial chromatic aberration, vignette,
  midtone-weighted grain, highlight shoulder and dithered 8-bit output. All of it is one fused Numba kernel.

## 4. Sound design (no music)

All effects are synthesized in `audio/synth.py`.

* **Keyboard "creamy thock":** damped modal body at 205–245 Hz with 2.7× and 6× partials, a low-passed thud and a
  barely-there tick, plus a softer top-out 55–80 ms later. The space bar sits lower (150–170 Hz). Spectral
  centroid is about 416 Hz.
* **Text appearance "cream" pop:** a 300→130 Hz pitch-drop sine with a breathy low-passed air layer and a 62 Hz sub
  tap, through a soft plate. Centroid is about 428 Hz.
* **Signature effects:** the blast (inhale, crack, a 52→22 Hz saturated sub glide, brown-noise body, distant
  crackle), heartbeat for the warm core, blizzard (decorrelated gusting wind with faint whistle), avalanche,
  split-flap clacks, Geiger ticks, telemetry pips, rope creak, snow footsteps, meltwater.
* **Mix:** voice chain (HPF 72 Hz, +1.8 dB warmth at 170 Hz, −1.5 dB de-box, static de-ess at 6.8 kHz, air shelf,
  2.6:1 compression, 5 % room), noise-only ambience beds per world, sidechain ducking, glue compression, a custom
  look-ahead true-peak limiter, **−14.0 LUFS integrated / −1.2 dBTP**.

## 5. Voice

Kokoro-82M, voice `af_heart` (Apache-2.0), generated line by line with phoneme overrides for *Nanda Devi*
/nˈʌndə dˈeɪvi/ and *Lop Nur* /lˈɔp nˈʊɹ/. Kokoro's own word timestamps drive every caption and visual beat.
Whisper small.en verifies every line: 0 word errors (only spelling variants such as "Lopnor" and "60").
Chatterbox was evaluated, but it produced NaN audio on this CPU.

## 6. Tech stack and rebuild

Python 3.12 · skia-python (vector, text, SkSL-capable raster) · HarfBuzz shaping · NumPy/SciPy/Numba ·
OpenCV · AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM-derived DEM, z7/z12/z13) · Natural Earth rivers · pedalboard · pyloudnorm · FFmpeg/x264.

```
python tools/fetch_dem.py                 # terrain
python vo/make_vo.py   (Kokoro venv)      # narration + word timings
python render.py video                    # picture (2 workers)
python audio/mix.py                       # sound
ffmpeg -i out/video_noaudio.mp4 -i out/master.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 320k out/final.mp4
```
