# Image-generation prompts (optional photoreal plates)

Everything in the delivered film was produced in code. These plates are **optional upgrades** where photographic
realism could add texture that procedural graphics can't fully match. Each prompt names the exact slot in the
timeline, so a plate can be dropped in without re-cutting.

**Global rules for every image:**
- 9:16, 2160×3840 (or the model's nearest), no text, no logos, no watermarks, no borders.
- Palette lock: shadows #06080B→#18212B, highlights #E8EDF1, a single warm accent #FFB04A where specified. No
  other saturated colours.
- Film character: fine 35 mm grain, gentle halation on highlights, no HDR look, no oversharpening.
- Keep the top 30 % of the frame (y < 580 px at 1080×1920) calm and low-detail. Captions live there.
- Must not depict identifiable real people. Anything that looks like a photo of the 1965 team must be captioned
  in-film as "ILLUSTRATION". Never present a generated image as archival.

### P1 — Nanda Devi under moonlight (alternate opener, 0.0–1.2 s, or behind the 3D ridge shot at 23.5 s)
> Ultra-wide vertical landscape photograph of Nanda Devi, the twin-summited Himalayan peak, seen from the
> south-west across the Nanda Devi Sanctuary at 2 a.m. under a three-quarter moon. The main summit is sharp and
> pyramidal at frame centre, two-thirds down the frame. The lower east summit sits to its right, joined by a
> long corniced ridge. Snow faces are lit cold silver-blue (#AEB8C2) and the rock bands are near-black (#0D1218).
> The sky is deep blue-black (#06080B) with sparse faint stars and no Milky Way. Thin spindrift streams off the
> summit ridge to the left. Shot on a 35 mm lens at f/8, ISO 3200, 20-second exposure, slight star trails shorter
> than 3 px. The foreground is a dark glacier with soft moonlit sastrugi. Quiet, ominous, documentary realism.
> **Negative:** people, tents, lights, aurora, purple sky, oversaturated blue, lens flare, text.

### P2 — the generator in the ice, macro (replaces the procedural macro for 0.0–1.1 s)
> Extreme macro, cinematic CGI cross-section: a cylindrical SNAP-style radioisotope generator, about 50 cm across,
> seen end-on, embedded deep in ancient glacial ice. Six thin radial steel cooling fins. A ring of zig-zag
> thermocouple junctions in dull copper surrounds a graphite block holding seven capsules in a hexagonal pattern
> (one centre, six around). The capsules glow from within, warm amber #FFB04A shading to a pale core #FFE9C2. The
> glow softly lights the surrounding ice and reveals fine annual ice layers running diagonally, tiny trapped air
> bubbles and hairline fractures. A thin dark meltwater halo surrounds the device. Everything outside the glow
> falls to blue-black #0D1218. Shallow depth of field (100 mm macro, f/2.8), light volumetric scattering in the
> ice, subtle chromatic aberration at the edges.
> **Negative:** radiation symbols, green glow, sci-fi panels, text, cartoon style.

### P3 — the cache in the storm (behind the crevice shot, 44.5–47.9 s)
> Night, close and low on a 7,300 m Himalayan ridge in a blizzard. A dark granite crack slants diagonally through
> frame centre. Wedged into it is a 1960s expedition load: two olive-grey steel radio cases with round dials, a
> finned cylindrical generator, and a folded antenna strapped on top. All of it is lashed with orange hemp climbing
> rope to two steel pitons. Wind-driven snow streaks diagonally from upper right to lower left with long
> motion-blur trails. A faint warm amber glow (#FFB04A) leaks from between the generator fins and is the only warm
> light. Headlamp-free; ambient light is a cold blue-grey whiteout. Photographic, 28 mm, f/2, 1/30 s.
> **Negative:** people, faces, logos, modern gear, bright colours, text.

### P4 — the same crack in spring, empty (48.0–50.2 s, must match P3's framing exactly)
> Identical camera position and framing to P3, but cold clear dawn in May. Pale blue-grey light (#2F3A46 → #6B7784),
> no falling snow. The crack is empty, with fresh scoured snow and fractured rock below it as if the slope has
> slid. No rope, no equipment. Long faint shadows. Photographic, 28 mm, f/8.
> **Negative:** any equipment, people, warm light, text.

### P5 — Lop Nur, the Tarim desert at night (behind the map flash, 12.7–14.3 s, low opacity)
> Aerial oblique photograph from 30 km altitude over the dry Lop Nur basin in Xinjiang at night. Flat, cracked
> salt crust with faint concentric "ear" patterns of the dried lake in deep grey-blue (#0D1218). On the far horizon
> a single intense white-orange flash point lights the haze from below, casting long low-angle light across the
> crust ridges. No visible cloud shape and no mushroom cloud: only the light. Thin atmospheric haze, documentary
> realism, 50 mm equivalent.
> **Negative:** mushroom cloud, fire, cities, roads, text.

### P6 — 1978 newsprint texture (under the clipping at 58.9 s)
> Top-down scan of yellowed 1970s newsprint at 1200 dpi. Fine fibres, faint ink set-off from the reverse page,
> slightly uneven grey halftone, a soft fold crease running diagonally. Neutral off-white #EDE9DF with
> barely-visible warm stains, evenly lit with no shadows. **Must contain no legible text or mastheads.**
> **Negative:** readable words, logos, photos, colour cast.
