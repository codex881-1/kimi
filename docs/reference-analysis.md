# Reference-film analysis

The three supplied films were inspected through metadata, full-duration contact
sheets and individual frames. Times below follow the three-second sampling grid.

## `igexport-DerOn9PoOcl.mp4` — 66.20 s, 720×1280, 24 fps

A space-infrastructure explainer built on an ivory/aubergine split-screen system.
At 00–15 s, repeated launch vehicles become a globe, map and rocket cutaway. At
18–30 s, a satellite is disassembled into power, cooling, radiation and workload
requirements, then multiplied into an orbital network. At 30–48 s, server racks,
a balance scale and payload figures turn abstract capacity into physical weight.
The final section returns to launch vehicles and an orbital constellation.

Useful techniques: persistent visual metaphors; objects carried across cuts;
large numerals paired with a physical analogy; alternating dense and empty
frames. Limitations to surpass: many illustrations remain generic isometric
icons, the split screen is reused mechanically, and some text holds have little
secondary motion. Our film instead gives each mechanism its own geometry and
uses the same film pod to connect the camera, recovery plan and failures.

## `igexport-DeOyd3hoMTL.mp4` — 67.71 s, 720×1280, 30 fps

A venture-capital explainer using ivory, purple and metallic gold. At 00–15 s,
photographed hands and money become an hourglass of coins. At 15–33 s, carts,
chips and money containers explain market concentration. At 30–45 s, watering
coins and an expanding pile turn capital flows into a growth metaphor. At
45–66 s, barbells, towers, a bull and a drought metaphor deliver the conclusion.

Useful techniques: each sentence receives a concrete visual verb; numerical
claims arrive inside the metaphor; recurring objects maintain continuity.
Limitations to surpass: the metaphors sometimes replace the underlying mechanism,
and mixed stock/3D assets vary in material quality. Our reconstructions prioritize
causal mechanics over metaphor and use one consistent material and lighting model.

## `ssstwitter.com_1793036819317.mp4` — 33.83 s, 910×512, 30 fps

A landscape fintech product film. At 00–06 s, a refractive brand object emerges
from colored light and glass. At 06–15 s, product interfaces, currency and a
phone appear in bright, sparse layouts. At 15–27 s, globe, account and phone
components connect the product story. At 27–34 s, prismatic flares resolve into
the brand mark.

Useful techniques: controlled specular highlights, shallow visual depth, polished
product reveals and a clean brand resolve. Limitations to surpass: the interface
showcase explains little causality, and flare-driven transitions would become
visual noise in a historical documentary. Our film borrows the material restraint,
not the glow-heavy transition language.

## Creative response

The new film keeps the references’ strongest short-form principle—one idea per
shot—but replaces repeated templates with a continuous mechanical narrative.
Deep keyboard thocks belong only to typed evidence; softer tactile taps accompany
major text. Mechanical effects are synchronized to visible parts. No music,
generic riser, impact, whoosh or lens-flare layer is used to manufacture excitement.
